TAP-TO-WI-FI JOURNEY

Tap. View. Connect. Measure.

The NFC point is free to the venue. Customers tap it, see an eligible advertiser and continue into the venue's existing guest Wi-Fi route.

01

Tap

Customer taps the free Elevation Media NFC wall or bar mount.

02

View

An eligible sponsor is selected from the active campaign pool for that location.

03

Engage or skip

The customer can visit the advertiser or continue towards guest Wi-Fi.

04

Connect

Portal venues hand customers into the venue portal. Password venues show the SSID with one-tap password copy.

05

Measured

The interaction is recorded against campaign, venue and area.
